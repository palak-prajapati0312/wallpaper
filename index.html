THE OCEAN

The ocean covers more than 70% of our planet and is home to countless species of plants and animals. It provides us with food, oxygen, and even helps control the Earth’s climate. Without the ocean, life on Earth would not be possible. 🐠 Ocean Life🐬 From tiny plankton to giant whales, the ocean is full of amazing creatures. Coral reefs are often called the “rainforests of the sea” because they support so many different species. Every living thing in the ocean plays an important role in keeping the ecosystem balanced. 🌱 Caring for the Ocean Simple actions like reducing plastic use, keeping beaches clean, and supporting conservation efforts can make a big difference.
